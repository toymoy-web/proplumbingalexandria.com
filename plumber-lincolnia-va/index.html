Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Lincolnia, Alexandria

Lincolnia is a census-designated place in eastern Fairfax County, just west of the City of Alexandria and east of Annandale. About 22,900 people lived there at the 2020 census, in more than 8,500 housing units. Mailing addresses use Alexandria, VA and ZIP 22312.

Little River Turnpike (Route 236) runs through the center, with Columbia Pike and Holmes Run along the north. Interstate 395 reaches the area at Exit 2 on Edsall Road. The western edge follows Edsall Road, Clifton Street, Braddock Road, and Little River Turnpike.

Lincolnia began after the Civil War as a community of freed slaves and remained one of Fairfax County's original African American communities for more than a century. Its segregation-era schoolhouse survives as part of the Lincolnia Senior Center. The City of Alexandria's own planning list also names a Lincolnia neighborhood, so jurisdiction can depend on the exact address.
