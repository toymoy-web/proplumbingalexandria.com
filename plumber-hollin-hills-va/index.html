Before Help Arrives

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

A burst pipe in a single-family house can do a lot of damage before anyone is home to notice it. Here is what to do while help is on the way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Close the main shutoff to stop the flow before anything else. Find it now, while nothing is wrong, so you know it works.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

If you can reach the panel safely, switch off the circuits serving the wet room. Stay out of standing water near live outlets.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables out of the wet space, and open a door if water is spreading.

Call Our 24/7 Line

Call the emergency line. Dispatch is answered around the clock, so there is no bad hour to call.

Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Hollin Hills, Alexandria

Hollin Hills is a mid-century modern neighborhood in the Fort Hunt area of southeastern Fairfax County, with Alexandria, VA mailing addresses. Developer Robert Davenport and architect Charles Goodman planned it, and homes were built between 1949 and 1971 on about 326 acres. There are more than 450 houses.

The neighborhood was listed on the Virginia Landmarks Register and the National Register of Historic Places in 2013. Fairfax County designated a Historic Overlay District in 2022, which gives the county's Architectural Review Board a say in some projects such as demolitions and additions.

Residents care for more than 30 acres of neighborhood parks, including Goodman Park, Voigt Park, and Brickelmaier Park. Fort Hunt Road and Stafford Road are the main routes through the area, and the mid-century style is protected by architectural covenants.

What Plumbing Calls Look Like Here

Covenants and Review

Exterior work such as a new vent, an addition, or relocated equipment may need approval through the community covenants or the county. Ask before you plan it.

Remodels and Additions

Houses that are more than fifty years old have usually been updated in pieces, so the plumbing can be a mix of original and newer work. A camera or pressure check shows what is actually there.

Landscaping Near the Lines

Landscaping is part of the Hollin Hills design, and tree roots near a sewer line are a common cause of slow drains in any older neighborhood. A camera inspection shows whether roots are involved.

Water Heater Planning

A tank of any age fails eventually. Replacing it on your schedule beats replacing it after it floods a floor.
