Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Arlandria, Alexandria

Arlandria is the northeast corner of the city, a compact neighborhood along Mount Vernon Avenue where Alexandria meets Arlington at Four Mile Run. It takes its name from the Arlandria Shopping Center, which opened in 1947, and much of the housing dates to the late 1930s and the years after World War II. Garden apartments such as Presidential Gardens, built in 1939, share the streets with rowhouses and small single-family homes.

The Birchmere music hall on Mount Vernon Avenue is the landmark most visitors know, and Four Mile Run Park runs along the northern edge. Hume Springs, a small neighborhood of early-1950s homes, borders the park just to the east.

Local Plumbing Challenges

Late-1930s Garden Apartments

Buildings from the late 1930s and 1940s can still carry original cast-iron drain stacks and galvanized supply lines that fail one unit at a time.

Four Mile Run Flood History

Lower Four Mile Run flooded repeatedly through the 1960s and early 1970s, and the city has since added culverts and check valves to its storm drains.

Shared Stacks Between Units

In multi-unit buildings a leak in one kitchen can show up in the unit below, so diagnosis starts with tracing the shared stack.

Backups After Heavy Rain

When storm drains run full, floor drains and sewer laterals can back up, which is a different problem from a failed pipe and needs a camera to confirm.

Before You Call

What Arlandria Homeowners Should Know

Tell Us Which Unit Is Wet

If you rent or own in a multi-unit building, note which units sit above and below the leak. It helps the technician trace the shared stack faster and gives your property manager something concrete.

Find Your Unit Shutoff

Older garden apartments often have a shutoff under the sink or behind the toilet. Locate it before an emergency so you can stop water at your own unit.
