Replacing a water heater is a bigger decision than repairing one, since the choice made now, tank size, tankless conversion, venting type, usually determines how the household's hot water performs for the next 10 to 15 years. This team walks through sizing and venting specifics before any install begins, rather than defaulting to swapping in the same size unit that failed. That conversation alone often changes the outcome, since a household that has grown since the original unit was installed may need more capacity than a like-for-like swap would provide.

Local Cause

Common Reasons Alexandria Homeowners Replace Instead of Repair

A tank over 10 years old with visible corrosion or a base leak is rarely worth repairing again. Beyond age, some Alexandria households replace proactively when renovating an older Rosemont or Del Ray kitchen or bath, since a full repipe often happens alongside a water heater upgrade to the same modern standard.

Signs to Watch For

When Replacement Makes More Sense Than Repair

Tank Age Over 10 Years

Most manufacturers rate tanks for 8 to 12 years. Past that point, a failure is a matter of when, not if.

Repeated Repairs

A second or third repair call within a single year usually costs more over time than one full replacement.

Household Growth

A tank sized for a smaller household runs out of hot water faster as more people or fixtures are added over time.

Wanting Tankless

Homeowners tired of running out of hot water often ask about a tankless conversion during a planned replacement visit.

How We Diagnose It

How We Size a New Unit

Sizing depends on household size, peak simultaneous hot water demand, and existing venting, not just matching the old tank's gallon rating. For a tankless conversion, the technician also checks gas line sizing and venting requirements, since older Alexandria homes sometimes need updated venting to support a tankless unit's higher output.

Repair Options

Tank vs. Tankless

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simpler installation
  • Limited hot water supply
  • 10-15 year lifespan

Tankless

  • Endless hot water on demand
  • Lower monthly energy cost
  • Compact wall-mounted unit
  • 20+ year lifespan
