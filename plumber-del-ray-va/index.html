If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

Del Ray's mix of original bungalow plumbing and Four Mile Run drainage means quick action helps. Here's what to do while help is on the way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source first, before anything else.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

If it's safe to reach, cut power to any outlet or panel near standing water.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the affected space.

Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Del Ray, Alexandria

Founded in 1894 as the Town of Potomac, annexed by Alexandria in 1930. A streetcar-era commuter suburb built around Mount Vernon Avenue, now a walkable commercial corridor. That streetcar-era history is exactly why plumbing here varies so much from one block to the next: a bungalow renovated in the last decade may already run modern PEX, while its neighbor from the same construction year still has the original galvanized supply line.

ZIP Coverage

Del Ray ZIP 22301 & 22305 Coverage

Del Ray spans both ZIP 22301 and ZIP 22305, and this team dispatches to both daily. Homes in ZIP 22301, closer to Mount Vernon Avenue's original commercial corridor, call most often for faucet and sink repair and drain cleaning in older bungalow kitchens. The 22305 side, nearer Four Mile Run, sees more water heater repair and general plumbing repair calls tied to renovated units mixing old and new pipe. Same-day service applies across both ZIPs, and technicians carry fittings for both original galvanized pipe and modern PEX to handle either scenario in one visit.
