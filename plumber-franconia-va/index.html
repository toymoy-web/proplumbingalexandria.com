Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Franconia, Alexandria

Franconia is a southeastern Fairfax County community southwest of Alexandria, with mailing addresses in ZIP 22310. It began as a railroad stop in 1871, when a station took its name from Frankhonia Farm, and regular service ended around 1953. Suburban growth followed from the 1950s, including Rose Hill, whose first house went up in 1954.

Franconia Road now runs past the Franconia Governmental Center and the Franconia Museum at 6121 Franconia Road on its way to the Franconia-Springfield Metro station, the last stop on the Blue Line. Springfield Town Center, which began as Springfield Mall in the early 1970s, sits just west.

Local Plumbing Challenges

Houses From the 1950s and 1960s

Rose Hill and nearby subdivisions date to the 1950s and 1960s, so original cast-iron drains and older copper supply lines are still in service in some homes.

Water Heaters Past Their Years

Tanks installed during earlier renovations are now at or beyond the typical 8 to 12 year life.

Worn Shutoff Valves

Original angle stops under sinks and behind toilets often seize or leak when turned, so a simple fixture swap can turn into a valve replacement.

Fixtures Wearing Out Together

Disposals, faucets, and toilets wear out on predictable timelines, and a refresh often exposes the aging parts behind them.

Before You Call

What Franconia Homeowners Should Know

Replace Old Shutoffs While You're There

If a faucet or toilet is being swapped, ask for the shutoff valve to be replaced in the same visit. It adds little cost and removes a common failure point.

Check the Tank's Date Label

Rust at the fittings, rumbling noises, and water pooling at the base all point toward replacement. Catching it early avoids a flooded utility room.
