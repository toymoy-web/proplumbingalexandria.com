If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

West End's mix of garden apartments and single-family homes means quick action limits the damage. Here's what to do while help is on the way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source first, before anything else.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

If it's safe to reach, cut power to any outlet or panel near standing water.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the affected space.

ZIP Coverage

West End ZIP 22311 & 22312 Coverage

West End spans ZIP 22311 and ZIP 22312, and both are dispatched to daily. Garden apartment complexes throughout both ZIPs generate the most water heater and multi-unit drain calls, while the newer townhome infill pockets, mostly in ZIP 22312, see more standard fixture installs and warranty-period repairs. Homes closer to the Holmes Run and Cameron Run corridors in either ZIP benefit from a sump system check ahead of heavy rain season.

What West End Residents Should Know
