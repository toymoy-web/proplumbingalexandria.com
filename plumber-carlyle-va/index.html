If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

In a Carlyle high-rise, a fast response limits how far a leak spreads through shared risers. Here's what to do while help is on the way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source first, before anything else.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

If it's safe to reach, cut power to any outlet or panel near standing water.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the affected space.

Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Carlyle, Alexandria

Alexandria's most contemporary neighborhood, built on former rail-yard land starting in the 1990s. Mixed-use, high-rise residential and office district. Local Plumbing Challenges

What Makes Carlyle's Plumbing Different

Shared Riser Systems

High-rise buildings route supply and drain lines through shared vertical risers, so a leak in one unit can affect the unit below.

HOA & Condo Coordination

Most repairs in Carlyle's high-rises require coordinating access with building management or an HOA before work can start.

All-Modern Plumbing

Built since the 1990s, Carlyle runs modern copper and PEX throughout, with no galvanized pipe to worry about.

Mixed-Use Density

Residential units share buildings with office space and retail, which sometimes means coordinating shutoffs across multiple tenant types.

ZIP Coverage

Carlyle ZIP 22314 Coverage

All of Carlyle sits within ZIP 22314, alongside the adjacent blocks of Old Town. High-rise leak detection is the most requested service in this ZIP, given how many residential towers and mixed-use buildings sit close together here. Technicians dispatched to ZIP 22314 are experienced navigating building management check-in procedures and shared-riser access, which speeds up same-day scheduling compared to a standalone single-family home.

Before You Call

What Carlyle Residents Should Know

Check Your Building's Access Policy

Some Carlyle buildings require advance notice or a front-desk check-in for contractor visits, worth confirming before scheduling so the visit isn't delayed at the lobby.

Report Shared-Wall Leaks Promptly

A leak affecting a neighboring unit should be reported to building management alongside your plumbing call, since coordination speeds up the fix and limits damage to both units.

Keep Building Contact Information Handy

Having your building's property management contact ready when you call speeds up scheduling, since most Carlyle repairs require that coordination before a technician can access shared spaces or riser closets.
