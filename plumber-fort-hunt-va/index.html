Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Fort Hunt, Alexandria

Fort Hunt is the southeastern Fairfax County community around Fort Hunt Road and the George Washington Memorial Parkway, with Alexandria mailing addresses in ZIPs 22308 and 22307. It is named for Fort Hunt, built on the Potomac in 1897 to defend Washington, D.C., and Fort Hunt Park, run by the National Park Service, marks the site.

Neighborhoods include Waynewood, Hollin Hall Village, Tauxemont, Stratford Landing, Collingwood, and Plymouth Haven. Listings in Hollin Hall Village show brick ramblers built in 1951 and 1955, typical of the era. The Hollin Hall Shopping Center on Fort Hunt Road anchors the village, and Belle Haven Marina and the Mount Vernon Trail are minutes away.

ZIP Coverage

Fort Hunt ZIP 22308 and 22307 Coverage

Fort Hunt spans two ZIP codes, with most of the community in 22308 and parts, including Villamay, Marlan Forest, and much of Hollin Hills, in 22307. Every address in both ZIPs is on this team's routes, from Hollin Hall Village along Fort Hunt Road to the streets near the Potomac. Root-related clogs and aging supply lines are the usual reasons to call from the tree-lined streets, and remodeled kitchens and baths are a common source of drain complaints. Same-day and 24/7 emergency dispatch applies across both ZIPs. Permitted work in Fairfax County goes through Land Development Services and follows the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code (IPC).

Before You Call

What Fort Hunt Homeowners Should Know

Camera Before You Dig

If a drain keeps clogging, a camera inspection shows whether roots, a sag, or a broken joint is the cause before any repair is priced.

Check Venting Before Going Tankless

Converting a rambler to a tankless water heater means checking the venting path and gas supply first, since older houses were not built with it in mind.
