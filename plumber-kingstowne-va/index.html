Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Kingstowne, Alexandria

Kingstowne is a planned community in southeastern Fairfax County, centered on the intersection of South Van Dorn Street and Kingstowne Boulevard, with its own ZIP code, 22315. It was established in the mid-1980s and now has more than 5,000 homes, many of them townhouses built through the 1990s and early 2000s.

Neighborhoods in the ZIP include Kingstowne itself, Hayfield, Island Creek, and Windsor Estates. Kingstowne Towne Center anchors shopping and dining, and both the Van Dorn Street and Franconia-Springfield Metro stations are a short drive away. Listings describe community pools, tennis courts, and about 12 miles of walking trails.

Local Plumbing Challenges

Townhomes From the 1990s and 2000s

Homes built in 1993, 1995, and 2001 are now 25 to 33 years old, so original water heaters, toilets, and faucets are well past their expected life.

Three-Level Layouts

Bathrooms stack over each other, so a failed supply line upstairs can reach the finished level below.

Bathroom Remodels

Many resales include fully remodeled baths, and a remodel is the right time to replace old shutoffs and supply lines.

Shared Walls and HOA Rules

Townhomes share walls and sometimes drain connections, so a leak near a party wall may involve a neighbor and the association.
