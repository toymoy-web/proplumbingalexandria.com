Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Potomac Yard, Alexandria

A 7.5-million-square-foot mixed-use development on former rail-yard land, with its own Metro station opened May 19, 2023, and Virginia Tech's Innovation Campus anchoring the north end. As one of Alexandria's newest neighborhoods, Potomac Yard's plumbing profile looks more like Eisenhower East or Carlyle than the pre-war neighborhoods further south: new-construction installs and warranty-period fixes dominate, not aging-pipe repairs. With Virginia Tech's campus and the surrounding retail and residential blocks still actively under construction, this is one of the few Alexandria neighborhoods where the plumbing infrastructure itself is still being built out rather than aging in place.

Potomac Yard sits within ZIP 22301, shared with Del Ray. New-construction installs and warranty-period fixture repairs make up most of the calls from this development, concentrated in the residential blocks around Potomac Avenue and Main Line Boulevard. Before You Call

What Potomac Yard Residents Should Know

Check Your Builder's Warranty

As one of Alexandria's newest neighborhoods, many Potomac Yard units may still be within a builder's plumbing warranty period, worth confirming before scheduling a paid repair.

Note Riverfront Proximity

Ground-level units closest to the Potomac benefit from the same sump-system awareness as Old Town, even with modern stormwater design in place, particularly during major storm events.

Track Ongoing Construction Nearby

Active development around the Innovation Campus sometimes affects street access, so mentioning your exact address when scheduling helps avoid delays.
