Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Cameron Station, Alexandria

A 164-acre former U.S. Army quartermaster depot (established 1941), redeveloped starting in 1999 into a planned residential community of roughly 2,000 homes built in five phases. Now that the earliest homes here have passed the 25-year mark, this is one of the first Alexandria neighborhoods built entirely on modern plumbing to start seeing its first wave of major-appliance replacements, rather than the aging-pipe repairs common in the city's pre-war neighborhoods.

What Makes Cameron Station's Plumbing Different

All-Modern Construction

Built entirely since 1999, Cameron Station has no galvanized pipe or original cast-iron drains anywhere in the community.

The community's stormwater management was designed into the original 1999 development plan, reducing flood exposure compared to older neighborhoods.

Consistent Housing Types

Five phases of similar single-family, townhome, and condo construction mean plumbing issues tend to follow predictable patterns by phase.

ZIP Coverage

Cameron Station ZIP 22304 Coverage

Cameron Station sits within ZIP 22304, shared with neighboring Seminary Hill. Water heater replacement calls are concentrated in the community's earliest phases, built in 1999 and now over 25 years old, while the later phases completed in the early 2000s are just beginning to see similar replacement demand. Every phase of Cameron Station falls within this same ZIP, so response time doesn't vary by which phase your home belongs to.
