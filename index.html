Core Services

Plumbing Services in Alexandria, VA

Alexandria's housing stock runs from pre-1950s rowhouses in Old Town and Rosemont, still carrying galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains, to newer high-rise construction around Carlyle and Potomac Yard on modern PEX and copper. Both extremes call this network for different reasons: older homes for corroded valves, slab leaks, and sewer root intrusion, newer buildings for fixture installs, water heater sizing, and unit-to-unit leak troubleshooting in shared walls.

Most plumbing installations, replacements, and major repairs in the City of Alexandria require a permit through the Alexandria Department of Code Administration, and every Virginia plumber on this team holds a state-issued license through the Virginia Board for Contractors. If a job needs a permit pulled, we handle that as part of the job, not as a separate scramble on your end.

Before You Call

What to Check in a Plumbing Emergency

Know Your Main Shutoff

Locate the main water shutoff valve before an emergency happens, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the basement.

Shut Off, Then Call

For an active burst pipe, shut off the main valve first, then call our 24/7 line so the technician arrives ready to repair, not just assess.

Document for Insurance

Photograph standing water or damaged drywall before cleanup starts if you plan to file a homeowner's insurance claim.
