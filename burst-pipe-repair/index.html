A burst pipe is one of the few plumbing problems where minutes genuinely matter, since water keeps flowing until the line is shut off and repaired. This team runs a 24/7 emergency line specifically for active bursts, and dispatch reaches most Alexandria addresses within the hour.

Local Cause

Why Pipes Burst in Alexandria Homes

Older galvanized supply lines in Rosemont and Del Ray corrode from the inside over decades until a weak point finally gives way under normal water pressure, often without warning. Winter freeze events are the other major cause, since an uninsulated pipe run in an exterior wall or unheated crawlspace can freeze, expand, and crack even in a home with otherwise sound plumbing. A joint that was never properly sealed during a past renovation can also fail years later under normal pressure, unrelated to age or weather.

Signs to Watch For

Signs of a Burst Pipe

Sudden Loss of Water Pressure

A sharp, sudden pressure drop across the house often means a major line has failed somewhere.

Water Where It Shouldn't Be

Water pooling, dripping from a ceiling, or spraying from a wall is the clearest sign of an active burst.

Unexplained Wet Spots

A wet spot on a wall or ceiling with no obvious source can mean a burst pipe is running inside the structure.

Sound of Rushing Water

Hearing water running inside a wall with no fixture in use is a sign worth acting on immediately.

Water Actively Spraying or Pooling?

Shut off the main valve if you can, then call. Dispatch runs 24/7.

How We Diagnose It

What Happens When You Call

The technician confirms the main shutoff is closed if it isn't already, then locates the exact break point to assess whether a spot repair or a longer section replacement is needed. Most burst pipe repairs are completed the same visit, with water restored to the rest of the house as quickly as the repair allows. All repair work meets the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code's plumbing standards (IPC).

Repair Options

Spot Repair vs. Section Replacement

Spot Repair

  • Faster, lower cost
  • Fixes a single break point
  • Best for an isolated failure
  • Most common outcome

Section Replacement

  • Addresses a longer run of corroded pipe
  • Reduces risk of a repeat burst nearby
  • Recommended when surrounding pipe shows corrosion
  • Slightly longer repair time

Before You Call

What to Do Right Now

Shut Off the Main Valve

Locate and close the main water shutoff immediately to stop more water from entering the home.

Turn Off Electricity Near Water

If water is near an outlet or panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Photograph the Damage

Take photos of standing water and affected areas before cleanup starts, for insurance documentation.
