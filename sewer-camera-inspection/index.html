A sewer camera inspection sends a small camera through the sewer line to show exactly what condition the pipe is in, root intrusion, cracks, bellies, or full blockages, rather than guessing based on symptoms alone. This is often requested before buying an older Alexandria home, after a recurring backup, or before committing to a larger repair based on the actual footage rather than an estimate.

When You Need to See the Line, Not Just Clear It

A drain that keeps clogging despite repeated cabling is the most common reason people ask for this service, once it's clear the problem is coming back rather than being a one-time clog. A camera inspection shows whether that's root intrusion, a structural crack, or something else entirely, before spending more money on repeated temporary fixes.

Local Cause

Why This Matters for Alexandria's Older Homes

Pre-1960s homes throughout Rosemont, Del Ray, and parts of Old Town commonly still run on original clay or cast-iron sewer laterals, and a camera inspection is the only reliable way to see the actual condition of a pipe that's been underground for 60 or more years. Buyers of these older homes increasingly request this inspection specifically because a failing lateral is a real, costly repair that a general home inspection doesn't typically check.

Signs to Watch For

When a Camera Inspection Is Worth Requesting

Recurring Backups

A drain or line that clogs again within weeks of being cleared usually needs a camera to find the real cause.

Buying an Older Home

A pre-purchase inspection of the sewer lateral can reveal a costly repair before closing, not after.

Before a Major Repair

Confirming exactly what's wrong before committing to a repair avoids paying for the wrong fix.

Slow Drainage Sitewide

Multiple slow drains throughout the home can indicate a main line issue worth seeing directly.

How We Diagnose It

How the Inspection Works

A waterproof camera on a flexible cable is fed through an accessible cleanout or fixture drain, recording footage as it travels the length of the line to the city connection. The technician narrates findings in real time and provides the footage afterward, whether for your own records, an insurance claim, or a real estate transaction.
